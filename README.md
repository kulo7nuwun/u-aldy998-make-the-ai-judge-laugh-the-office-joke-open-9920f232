# Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open

One line of office comedy, written for
[Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open](https://make.fun/c/u-aldy998-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-the-office-joke-open-9920f232)
on make.fun.

## The entry

> I sent a reply-all asking everyone to stop replying-all, and now forty-seven people have agreed with me by replying-all.

The joke turns on reply-all: the attempt to end the thread is exactly
what feeds it, and the payoff lands in the last few words.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`. It needs no input,
no network, and no secrets — the line is written by hand in the source.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
